# Start here: Chickenshed NYC registration

A guide for Elaine, Elliot and the Chickenshed NYC team. Prepared for review • November 5, 2026.

## One place for new registrations

Families now use one registration page for Players, My Little Adventure, Mini Adventure, Labs and Summer Shed. They can register more than one child and choose more than one session before paying.

Start at the website’s Program Registration page. Use the current registration buttons rather than an old saved registration link.

## What a parent does

1. Sign in to the parent account. Add a Student Profile first if the child does not already have one.
2. Open Program Registration. Choose the Program, Workshop, Session and Student.
3. Choose Add Registration to include another child or session. Each row is one registration.
4. Check any blue notices. A child awaiting approval is marked Pending approval. The order shows that child’s deposit when one is assigned.
5. If needed, request financial aid and complete the questions. The price changes before checkout.
6. If How would you like to pay? appears, choose a payment option. It appears only when a selected session offers a choice.
7. Check Amount due today and the balance. Choose Continue to checkout. When no financial aid is applied, a family may enter a valid coupon at checkout.
8. Finish checkout and wait for the confirmation page. The family receives a registration confirmation and can see their registrations in the dashboard. A Stripe payment receipt is separate when a payment is taken.
## The payment choices

Pay in full: Pay the assigned full tuition. A Pending student may instead pay the assigned deposit while awaiting approval.

Pay per semester: Pay the assigned semester tuition, unless the student’s approval requires a deposit. Staff arrange any later semester payments. A later semester bill is not included in this order’s remaining balance.

Monthly payments: Pay each eligible registration’s assigned deposit now. This records the family’s preference. Staff must agree and arrange the later payments; the website does not start monthly charges.

A deposit is charged for each registration row that requires one. It counts toward that registration’s tuition. It is not one deposit for the whole family.

Short Mini Adventure blocks offer full payment, not monthly or semester plans. Their Pending students pay a $25 deposit per registration; Approved students pay the block’s tuition. Summer Shed keeps its own assigned deposit amount.

## Different items in the same checkout

A payment choice applies to sessions that offer that choice. A session offering only full payment keeps its full-payment price. A Pending student’s deposit still applies where assigned.

Example: An Approved child’s $175 Mini Adventure block plus a Players registration with a $25 monthly-arrangement deposit means $200 is due today, before any discounts. Later Players payments still need staff follow-up.

## Who can join a Lab?

The child taking the Lab must have an Active Players 2, 3 or 4 registration covering the Lab’s dates.

A family may add the qualifying Players registration in the same checkout for that same child, if it will become Active after checkout. This works for an Approved student.

An Approved student profile by itself does not qualify. A Pending Players registration does not qualify. A sibling’s Players registration does not qualify.

If the requirement is not met, the page explains which child needs a qualifying registration and blocks checkout. Labs follow the same available payment and financial-aid rules as other programs.

## Financial aid and coupons

Financial aid reduces tuition immediately, including eligible Lab tuition. It does not reduce deposits. The family’s submitted aid form is linked to the website order for staff to open.

Coupons are available at checkout when no financial aid is applied. A coupon may reduce tuition or deposits, as allowed by that coupon’s Stripe settings. Financial aid and a customer-entered coupon are not combined.

A 100% coupon can make today’s payment $0. If it covers only a deposit, tuition may still remain. A $0 checkout does not necessarily mean the whole order is paid off.

## What changed, and what staff still do

- New registrations share one page, one order and linked student registrations. Families no longer need a separate Lab checkout.
- New checkout does not create subscriptions. Existing subscriptions are preserved and still need their usual management.
- Approved students’ new registrations become Active after confirmed checkout. Pending students’ registrations remain Pending for staff review.
- Staff still review students, arrange later payments, collect those payments and keep the current balance up to date. Card details are saved through Stripe for monthly and semester arrangements when a card payment is collected; a $0 checkout may not save a new card.
